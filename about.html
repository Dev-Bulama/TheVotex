THE VISION

THEvotex was born from a single vision: to create Calgary's most immersive premium nightlife experience. We are not just a venue — we are a destination. A place where world-class sound, curated lighting, and flawless hospitality converge into something extraordinary.

Located in the heart of the city at 219 17 Avenue SW, THEvotex brings together the finest DJs, an elevated bottle service program, and an atmosphere that sets the standard for nightlife culture in Calgary. Whether you're celebrating, connecting, or simply living — your best night starts here.
